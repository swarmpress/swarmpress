swarm.press Bonsai runtime harness

Test page for the in-browser WebGPU model runtime (not part of the production build): Ternary Bonsai 2 27B through the pinned upstream engine, in the LLM worker and, for the equivalence check, on the main thread. The engine must be installed first (pnpm --filter @swarm-press/game bonsai:runtime); the first load downloads about 6 GB of weights. Driven by e2e/bonsai.spec.ts and e2e/bonsai-equivalence.spec.ts through window.__bonsai.
